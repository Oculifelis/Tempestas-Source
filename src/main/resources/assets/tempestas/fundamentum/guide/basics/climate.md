# Temperature and Humidity

Every spot in the world has a **temperature** and a **humidity**. The biome sets most of it. The season, the
height, the weather and nearby blocks push it up or down.

A **Climate Gauge** reads both where you stand. Sneak and use it to see each part on its own.
