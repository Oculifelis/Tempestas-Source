# The Weather Condenser

The condenser draws something out of the air every minute or so. What it makes depends on the climate where it
stands. Use it with an empty hand to take what it made, or pull from it with a hopper.

{{item tempestas:weather_condenser}}

- **Ember Salt** in hot, dry places like deserts and badlands
- **Rime Salt** in freezing places
- **Dew Pearls** in wet places like jungles and swamps
- **Storm Glass** under open sky in a thunderstorm, wherever it stands
