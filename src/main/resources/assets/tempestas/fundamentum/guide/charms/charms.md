# Charms

A charm is worn in the charm slot. Each one sleeps until its own weather is over you, and only works under open sky.

- **Ember Charm**, hot clear days and the Nether: you can't burn, and every hit sets the target alight.
- **Rime Charm**, snow: you never freeze, water freezes under your feet as you walk, and every hit freezes the target.
- **Dew Charm**, rain: you move faster and heal slowly, and crops and saplings around you grow faster.
- **Storm Charm**, thunder: lightning can't hurt you. A strike charges your next hit, which calls lightning down on
  the target. Hits sometimes call it anyway.

What each charm does, and in which weather, is datapack JSON (`tempestas/charms/`).
