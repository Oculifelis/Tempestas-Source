# Catching Skies

A **Sky Jar** catches the sky over the spot where it is opened. Not every sky can be caught: it needs a name in the almanac. Rain, snow, thunder, a clear day and a clear night always have one. Rarer skies need the right place, hour, height, moon or season.

A caught jar keeps where and when it was caught, and the first catch of each sky goes into the **Weather Almanac** for good. Skies still missing show a hint instead.

Opening a caught jar lets that sky out over a small patch for a while, and the empty jar comes back:

- **Rain** waters farmland and helps crops grow
- **Snow** settles and freezes still water
- **Thunder** calls down lightning
- **Clear skies** melt snow and ice, calm skies make hostile mobs lose interest

Some skies also give effects to anyone standing under them.

Every sky is a datapack entry in `tempestas/skies`, so packs can add new ones, change what they need or change what they let out.
